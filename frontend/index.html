About CareFirst AI

Why we built this

Emergency teams and families face the same problem from different sides: information about who can treat what, and where beds and OTs are free, is scattered across sheets, dashboards and phone calls — precisely when there's no time to search.

CareFirst AI started as a hackathon project (PS-01) focused on hospital monitoring search, and grew into a patient-facing tool with the same principle: turn a plain description of an emergency into a safe, evidence-backed match — fast enough to matter.

01

Built with real hospital operations patterns

Grounded in actual capacity and specialty structure, on synthetic demo records.
02

Designed for the worst moment, not the best

Large tap targets, short steps, works under stress.
03

Transparent by default

Every match shows its reasoning and evidence — never a black box.
